Generalization’s Computational Footprint

Optimization-based \min J(x, u)~~\text{s.t.} ~ g(x) = 0 RL a = \pi_\theta(s)
Online generalization Strong Weak
Computational requirement High Low
  • Online MPC -> NN distillation can be 10–70× (Khadiv et al., 2023) faster
  • NN fails to generalize when OOD or into unseen contact modes where online MPC would re-solve (Reske et al., 2021)
  • Model-based traj-opt inside RL training loop can be the primary bottleneck (Jenelten et al., 2024) (though not always (Bravo-Palacios et al., 2026)!)

DNN regularity allows codesign

  • TPU (Jouppi et al., 2017) – 30–80× perf/W
  • Serial Riccati recursion resists acceleration (Plancher & Kuindersma, 2018)

ASICs: performant, but inflexible

37 / 49

Computer Architecture Affects Throughput and Energy

Von Neumann vs. Dataflow

  • Stored-program bottleneck: Data movement costs 10× (SRAM) to >150× (DRAM) more energy than arithmetic (Horowitz, 2014)
  • MPC has low arithmetic intensity: Dominated by memory movement (e.g., TinyMPC (Nguyen et al., 2024))
  • Data-flow and domain flow: Organize computation around data movement (Omtzigt, 1990)

Flexible dataflow hardware is emerging

The opportunity for robotics:

  • Systolic arrays (Kung et al., 1979) unlocked DNNs
  • Domain-flow could unlock optimization-based control
38 / 49

Conclusion

What we want
  • Robots that exhibit dynamical stability and robustness
  • Can generate new behaviors online
  • Generalize to new tasks and environments
What we have so far
  • Ways to hand-craft composable dynamical primitives from templates
  • Ways to build RL primitives that may not be composable
What we need to build
  • Architectures that can flexibly and easily synthesize composable primitives
  • Architectures that can compose them online to generate new behaviors
